About CFG

A Johannesburg-based specialist protecting people, assets and infrastructure across South Africa.

01 Who we are

Built around safety and reliability

CFG Earthing and Lightning Protection Systems supplies practical, cost-effective protection against the effects of lightning and electrical faults. We work with clients from project planning through testing and maintenance.

Our team combines engineering support, proven installation methods and documented testing. Whether you need a new system or an independent check of an existing installation, we tailor our work to the site and the applicable project requirements.

02 What drives us

Vision, mission and workmanship

Our vision

To be one of South Africa’s most trusted earthing and lightning protection providers, known for reliable engineering and lasting client relationships.

Our mission

Protect lives, property and critical infrastructure through sound engineering, quality materials, safe and timely delivery, and open communication.

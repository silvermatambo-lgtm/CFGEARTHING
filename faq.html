Frequently asked questions

Does CFG work outside Johannesburg?

Yes. CFG is based in Newlands, Johannesburg and serves projects across South Africa. Share the site location so the team can confirm scheduling and scope.

Can CFG test an existing installation?

Yes. The scope can include lightning protection and down conductor inspections, earth resistance measurements, electrical continuity testing, system verification and documented results.

Does CFG assist with solar PV protection?

Yes. CFG offers earthing and lightning protection for ground-mounted and rooftop solar PV sites. The solution depends on the site, installation and applicable project requirements.

What should I send for a quotation?

Start with the project town or area, the type of site, the service you need and any deadline. Drawings, previous test reports or photos can help the team scope the work.

Does every test come with a compliance certificate?

CFG can provide testing records and technical reports. Certification depends on the agreed scope, inspection or test results, and applicable project requirements.

Can CFG repair or upgrade a system?

Yes. CFG offers preventative maintenance, repairs, component replacement, fault finding and upgrades for earthing and lightning protection systems.

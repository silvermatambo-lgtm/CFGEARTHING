02 Full capabilities

What each service covers

01

Lightning protection systems

Design support, supply and installation of external lightning protection for buildings, infrastructure and solar PV sites.

  • Lightning protection mast installation, replacement and upgrades
  • Air terminals, conductors and down conductors
  • Roof-mounted lightning protection systems
  • Lightning protection for solar PV installations
  • Structural lightning protection system upgrades
02

Earthing and grounding

Site-specific earthing systems designed to dissipate fault currents and bond equipment and structures.

  • Earthing design support and installation
  • Earth electrodes and rods
  • Earth grid installation and extensions
  • Equipment and structural bonding
  • Earthing upgrades and rehabilitation
  • Industrial and commercial grounding
03

Inspection, testing and certification

Inspection and performance verification for new and existing systems, with documented results and certification where applicable.

  • Soil resistivity testing
  • Earth resistance measurements
  • Electrical continuity and compliance testing
  • Lightning protection and down conductor inspections
  • Existing system verification and performance assessments
  • Technical reports, testing records and handover documentation
04

Lightning risk assessments

Site-specific assessments that identify exposure and help determine the right level of protection.

  • Site-specific technical evaluation
  • Assessment of existing lightning protection installations
  • Identification of lightning-related hazards and required protection levels
  • Review of project compliance requirements
  • Cost-effective mitigation recommendations and reports
05

Maintenance, repairs and upgrades

Routine maintenance and targeted repairs help keep protective systems working and reduce downtime.

  • Routine inspections and scheduled testing
  • Preventative lightning protection and earthing maintenance
  • Fault finding and replacement of damaged components
  • Rectification of installation deficiencies
  • Earthing improvements and compliance upgrades
06

Engineering support and consultancy

Practical technical input from early design and planning through on-site supervision and handover.

  • Earthing and lightning protection system design support
  • Technical site assessments
  • Installation specifications and technical recommendations
  • Project technical supervision
  • Assessment against applicable standards
  • Technical documentation and reporting
